# Survey design review

Use when the user asks how good the questionnaire is, why the data is hard to interpret, or what
to change next time. Also add 1–2 design notes to Caveats when design limits the analysis.

Check:
- **Missing segmenters** you'd need for the questions asked (plan, team, tenure, region).
- **Scales**: consistent direction and length; labelled endpoints; NPS uses 0–10.
- **Double-barrelled questions** ("fast and friendly support").
- **Leading wording** and social desirability (esp. HR surveys without anonymity assurance).
- **Optional questions** with very low answer rates — move earlier, make specific, or drop.
- **Open text**: one focused question ("What is the one thing we should improve?") yields more
  codable answers than "Any comments?".
- **Small populations** (teams < 5): plan reporting levels in advance; don't ask demographic
  questions that create identifiable cells.
- **Waves**: keep wording and scales identical, or comparisons break.
- **Completion time / attention checks** if data quality matters.

Give concrete rewrites, not just criticism.
